Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 15, 2025

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications, games, and related services published by Swiftmindapps (collectively referred to as the "Applications" or "Games"). It explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you install or play any of our Games on mobile platforms (such as Google Play Store or Apple App Store). By using our Games, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Summary — Short Version

We build our Games to be fun and privacy-friendly. Most of our Games do not require account creation and store progress locally on your device. We do not collect direct personal details (like your name or email) during normal gameplay unless you voluntarily contact support or enable account-based services. We may collect minimal non-personal identifiers and usage data to run analytics, fix crashes, and display advertisements (if enabled).

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the specific Game you play, we may collect and process the following types of information:

  • Local Game Data: Your game progress, scores, achievements, levels unlocked, and in-game settings are primarily stored locally on your device unless cloud sync/backup is explicitly enabled.
  • Device & Technical Data: Operating system version, device model, hardware specifications, app version, language/locale, and basic device performance metrics.
  • Identifiers: Unique device or advertising identifiers, such as the Google Advertising ID (GAID) or Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA). These are collected by third-party analytics and ad services.
  • Analytics & Diagnostics: Anonymous usage patterns, session lengths, feature interaction, and crash logs (via services like Firebase Crashlytics) to help us identify bugs and improve performance.
  • Advertising Data (if applicable): Third-party advertising networks may collect device identifiers and interaction data to serve, measure, and personalize ads within monetized Games.

3. How We Collect Information

  • Automatically: Technical device data, app performance metrics, and advertising IDs are automatically processed by embedded third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs) when you launch or play our Games.
  • Locally on Device: Saved game data and user settings remain stored locally on your physical device storage by default.
  • Cloud Sync & Account Integration (Optional): If a specific Game offers cloud backups, leaderboards, or online multiplayer features (e.g., Google Play Games Services, Game Center), progress data will be securely stored via these platforms subject to your explicit permission.

4. Purpose of Data Collection

We use the collected information for the following specific purposes:

  • To operate, deliver, and optimize gameplay functionality.
  • To monitor technical health, troubleshoot crashes, and improve overall game stability.
  • To display non-intrusive advertisements or personalized ads (where supported).
  • To support optional features like cloud save, achievements, and cross-device progress restoration.
  • To respond to customer support inquiries when you email us directly.

5. Third-Party SDKs and Services

Our Games may integrate third-party services that collect, monitor, and analyze data to help maintain and monetize our applications. Depending on the Game, integrated services may include:

  • Google Play Services / Apple Game Center: Platform integrations, achievements, and cloud saves.
  • Google Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics): Diagnostics, bug reporting, and aggregate usage telemetry.
  • Google AdMob / Unity Ads / AppLovin: Advertising distribution and performance tracking.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We only share aggregate, non-identifiable technical data with trusted service providers strictly necessary to operate, analyze, and monetize our Games. We may also disclose information if required by law or to defend our rights and platform safety.

7. Advertising & Opt-Out Options

For Games that display advertisements, third-party partners use ad identifiers to deliver relevant ads. You can control or opt out of personalized interest-based advertising directly from your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization (or Delete Advertising ID).
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Disable app tracking requests.

8. System Permissions

Our Games request only essential permissions necessary for core functionality. Standard permissions include:

  • INTERNET — Required to load online features, sync save data, load ads, and submit crash reports.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Used to check whether the device has an active internet connection.

If a specific Game requests optional runtime permissions (such as Storage access or Notifications), these will be requested interactively within the app, and you can grant or deny them at any time.

9. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Local game progress remains on your device until you clear the application data or uninstall the Game. Analytics and advertising data retained by third-party SDK partners are managed according to their respective privacy schedules.

To request data deletion: If you wish to request the removal of any data associated with your usage of our Games (or if you enabled cloud backups), please email us at swiftmindapps@gmail.com. We will process verifiable requests within a reasonable timeframe.

10. Children's Privacy

Our Games are designed for general audiences and are not directed towards children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected personal data from a child, please contact us immediately so we can promptly delete it.
